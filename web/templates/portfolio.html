Setup Steps

  1. Add your broker account using the form above
  2. Register for SnapTrade (free) at docs.snaptrade.com — get your Client ID and Secret
  3. Add credentials to your .env file: SNAPTRADE_CLIENT_ID and SNAPTRADE_CLIENT_SECRET
  4. Run setup: python data/fetch_portfolio.py setup — registers you with SnapTrade and gives you a login URL
  5. Connect your broker via the SnapTrade portal — your broker password goes to SnapTrade, not this app
  6. Sync — click "Sync Now" or wait for the nightly run
